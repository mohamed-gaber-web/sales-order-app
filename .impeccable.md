# Grow Path — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Field sales reps and operations staff who check stock levels and process purchase/sales orders while on the move. They use the app on mobile devices in fast-paced contexts — checking PO status between calls, confirming receipts at a loading dock, scanning orders in the field. Time-pressured, goal-oriented, rarely sitting at a desk.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy · Professional · Precise**

The app handles real business data — orders, quantities, money. Errors cost time and money. The interface must inspire confidence, never confusion. Think enterprise reliability with modern clarity — not cold or bureaucratic, but serious and dependable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Linear / Notion inspired — typography-led hierarchy, generous whitespace, minimal decoration. No visual noise. Every element earns its place. Navy (#002559) as the structural anchor; orange (#F24C1A→#F28E26) reserved exclusively for primary actions. Clean surfaces, strong type scale, purposeful motion.

**Anti-references**: Consumer apps, playful illustrations, decorative gradients, dashboard-for-dashboards-sake layouts.

### Copy Principles
- **Direct & practical**: Every word earns its place. Action verbs. No filler.
- **"Receive" not "Click here to receive this item"**
- **Status is always visible**: Users shouldn't have to wonder what happened
- **No jargon without context**: If a term comes from D365 (e.g. "Packing Slip"), it stays — but errors and UI labels use plain language
- **Errors are recoverable**: Never blame, always guide

### Design Principles
1. **Mobile-first, glanceable** — users are moving, not reading
2. **Every word earns its place** — cut ruthlessly, keep only what helps the user act
3. **Trust through clarity** — status, quantity, and outcome are always unambiguous
4. **Purposeful accent** — orange signals the one right action per screen
5. **Hierarchy over decoration** — spacing and weight do the visual work; ornament is earned
